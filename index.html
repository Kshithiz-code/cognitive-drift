How AI Dependency Reshapes Student Thinking

Artificial intelligence is transforming education faster than institutions can adapt. Students now solve assignments, coding problems, reports, and research tasks within seconds. While AI improves accessibility and efficiency, it also raises a deeper question:

Are students still learning — or simply generating answers?

The project

This Design Thinking project investigates the evolving relationship between students, faculty, and AI-assisted learning environments — examining how academic systems are adapting, or failing to adapt, to generative AI.

What We Explored

  • Why students increasingly depend on AI
  • How faculty perceive this behavioral shift
  • What changes occur in learning quality
  • Where traditional education systems struggle
  • How ethical AI-assisted learning can be encouraged
